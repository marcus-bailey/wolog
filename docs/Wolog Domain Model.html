How the pieces connect

Routine, Turn, Day

  • A Routine owns its Days. A Day never moves to another Routine; duplicate it instead.
  • The rotation is a list of Turns. Each Turn names one Day, so a Day can come up twice (A, B, A, C).
  • A Day with no Turns is off the rotation but still exists.
  • The next suggested Turn is computed from history. Nothing stores it.

Session and Snapshot

  • Saving the first Set creates the Session and copies the Day's Lines into it.
  • After that, the Session's Lines are its own. Editing the Day later changes nothing in past Sessions.
  • Deleting the last Set deletes the Session.
  • New Sessions and Skips only go to the Engaged Routine.

Exercise and Last-time

  • Day Lines and Session Lines both point at an Exercise in the library.
  • Last-time comes from the same Exercise in the most recent earlier Session that has a Set of it, on any Routine or Day.
  • Retired Exercises keep their history and their Last-time.
